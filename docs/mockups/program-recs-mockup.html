Program Recs

The AI stops editing your program at you mid-chat. Instead it stages a Program Rec — a ride-along surface like the workout log: a bar at the bottom, a sheet that slides up, an ✕ that parks it. Open it on your own time and see exactly which lines change, side by side, widen or shrink the change yourself, and decide how long it lives.

Field Mode and PR propagation stay as they are. This replaces the check-in injury card and the pain / plateau / equipment mid-chat offers — and it becomes the mastermind's one and only way to touch a program.

✓ Stays as-is

  • Field Mode — temp program for hotel gyms, auto, expires on its own.
  • PR propagation — a new PR quietly moves that lift's reference max, nothing else.

→ Becomes a Program Rec

  • Check-in injury tweak — no more apply-card at the end of the check-in.
  • Pain / plateau / equipment offers — no more mid-chat staging right after a log.
  • Mastermind program edits — the new program tool can only ever stage a Rec, never write directly. "Swap Thursday with what I logged today" lands here.

1 The nudge, not the interruption

Joe mentions it. The Rec waits.

No apply-card in the middle of the thread, and never while a workout bar is up. Joe drops one line — "check the program rec I made to help out that left pec" — and the Rec bar slides in at the bottom, same family as the workout bar and program-draft bar.

The Rec bar is the white/outlined variant so it never fights the navy workout bar when they stack. ✕ parks it to Past Blocks (scene 5). It survives app restarts until you deal with it.

Check-ins end the same way: the check-in wraps normally, and the Rec is waiting at the bottom instead of gating the close.

2 The Rec sheet

Only the lines that change — before over after, reason beside.

Tap the bar and the sheet slides up under the header, same maneuver as the log sheet. It never shows the full program: the left column lists each exact program line being replaced (clay, struck through) with its replacement directly beneath (navy). Tap any replacement to edit it in place, or use the composer below — "tell Joe what to change" revises the Rec, not the chat.

The right rail is the case for the change, quoting what you actually said, plus How long — the answer to "changes feel permanent when they shouldn't be." A Rec applied "until the pec clears" gets tracked at check-ins and Joe proposes the revert the same way.

Apply is surgical: only the struck lines are swapped, character-for-character, in your saved program. No AI rewrite of the whole document at apply time — what you see is exactly what lands, and the Program tab reflects it immediately.

3 You control the blast radius

Widen it, shrink it, add to it.

The AI proposed the smallest change; you disagree with the size, not the idea. Tap Whole day and the highlight grows — Joe re-drafts replacements for the newly included lines and the WHY updates to match. + Add another swap lets you point at any other program line (or add a brand-new line, shown here) and pull it into the same Rec.

Scope is also conversational: "widen this to the whole day" in the composer does the same thing. Chips for thumbs, composer for nuance.

4 Mastermind-native

Asked-for edits take the same door.

This is the mastermind's program tool (the P2v2 write-tool). It can read your log — sets, reps, loads — and draft the change, but the tool's only power is staging a Rec. It can never write the program directly. Whether Joe volunteers a change or you demand one, everything funnels through the same reviewable surface.

That's the confidence answer: one door, always visible, always inspectable line-by-line before anything lands. It also means one code path to test instead of five.

Decisions needed

Seven questions before this gets built

Q1 · TIMING

When exactly does the bar drop? Never mid-workout is settled — but is it (a) immediately after the log/check-in wraps, (b) held until the NEXT app open, or (c) case-by-case (injury recs right away, plateau/equipment recs next open)?

My rec: (a) for injury — the context is fresh and it's safety-relevant — and (c)'s deferral for plateau/equipment, which have no urgency.

Q2 · DURATION

Does every Rec carry a "How long" (until-cleared / N weeks / permanent) with Joe proposing the revert as a new Rec when the condition lifts? And does "until cleared" get judged by check-in answers, or by you saying so in chat?

My rec: yes on every Rec, defaulting per trigger (injury → until cleared, plateau → rest of block, explicit asks → permanent). Check-in answers AND chat both count as the clearing signal.

Q3 · COACH-LOCKED ATHLETES

Today a locked athlete's pain flag files a change request to the coach. In the new world, does the athlete see the same Rec sheet read-only ("sent to Coach Reed") while the coach keeps the staged-merge panel — or does the coach's surface get rebuilt as this same side-by-side too?

My rec: athlete sees the Rec read-only with its status; rebuilding the coach panel onto this component is a fast follow, not v1.

Q4 · PHONE LAYOUT

The side-by-side (swaps left, WHY right) is drawn exactly as you described. On a 390-point phone that means small type in the WHY rail. Alternative: WHY as a collapsible strip above the swaps that opens full-width. Keep the true side-by-side, or stack it?

My rec: keep side-by-side — the reasoning being visible WHILE you read the swap is the point — but let the WHY rail collapse to a ⓘ tab once read.

Q5 · EDITS ARE FINAL

When you edit a replacement line directly, apply uses your text verbatim — no AI cleanup pass. When you widen scope, Joe re-drafts only the newly included lines and never touches ones you already hand-edited. Confirm both?

My rec: yes to both — hand edits are sacred, and apply is a deterministic line-swap so what you approved is byte-for-byte what lands.

Q6 · HOW MANY AT ONCE

Can two Recs be live at once (bars stack like the workout + program bars do), or is it one active Rec with the rest parked in Past Blocks automatically?

My rec: one bar on screen max; a second Rec auto-parks with a one-line note from Joe. Keeps the bottom of the screen calm.

Q7 · EXPIRY

Do parked Recs go stale — auto-archived when the block they reference ends (their line references may no longer exist) — or live forever until you delete them?

My rec: a Rec whose target lines vanish (block ended, program rewritten) gets marked "outdated" but stays visible — Joe can re-draft it against the new program in one tap.
